About Me

I’ve always been drawn to creativity and finding new ways to solve problems. That made design a natural path for me when choosing a direction in life. I enjoy thinking freely, exploring ideas, and discussing solutions together with others.

Choosing the digital field came from my interest in how technology shapes society, but also from seeing how digitalisation can fall short when it isn’t thoughtfully executed. This has motivated me to create digital solutions that truly work for users in their context - which to me is good design.

Expanding my skills into full‑stack development is my way of understanding and contributing to the entire process, from idea to finished product - and because I enjoy it. I also believe that design and development will continue to move closer together, making it valuable to understand both sides.

I enjoy working in teams, especially with agile methods, and I appreciate when different perspectives come together. As a person, I’m structured, organised, and like to keep things moving forward, while also valuing collaboration.

DESIGN STUDIO

Strength as a Service

Two services, one for renting training equipment and one for collecting training equipment - enable ELEIKO to transition from a linear to a circular business model.

CLIENT

ELEIKO

DURATION

8 weeks

MY ROLE

Researcher, Service designer

METHODS

Interviews, analysises, stakeholder map, customer journey map, service blueprint, storyboards.

GOAL

Help Eleiko transition from a linear to a circular business model by offering rental of training equipment.

Background & motivation

Client

This project was carried out in collaboration with Eleiko, a leading provider of strength training equipment that is actively exploring more sustainable and circular business opportunities.

Problem Statement

Eleiko wanted to transition from a linear to a more circular business model. At the same time, customers face high costs when purchasing equipment, while Eleiko has limited control over the circulation of products after sale.

This project explored how a service could be designed to enable customers to rent training equipment while providing Eleiko with valuable insights into product usage. The aim was to support a transition towards a more circular business model by extending product lifecycles, improving resource utilization, and increasing control over the circulation of equipment.

Circular Business Model

A circular business model aims to maximize the value and lifespan of products by enabling reuse, refurbishment, and recirculation. For Eleiko, this meant exploring ways to maintain ownership and control of equipment throughout its lifecycle rather than relying solely on product sales.

My Contribution

Throughout the project, I contributed to understanding both user and business needs by conducting interviews, analyzing insights, and mapping the customer journey. I also helped translate research findings into service concepts through service blueprints and storyboards, ensuring that proposed solutions were both user-centered and aligned with Eleiko's business goals.

Design Process

Preparatory Work

Preparatory work was conducted to understand Eleiko's current situation, sustainability goals, and the challenges of implementing a circular business model. This helped identify opportunities and establish a solid foundation for developing relevant and realistic service concepts.

Domain Knowledge

I acquired domain knowledge by desktop research to ensure I had an understanding to deliver an appropriate service for the Eleiko, that could give their customers a possibility to rent training equipment and make them go from a linear to a circular business model.

SWOT Analysis

A SWOT analysis was conducted to gain a better understanding of Eleiko's current position and its ability to transition towards a circular business model. The analysis highlighted key factors that could influence the development of future service concepts.

Sustainability Analysis

A sustainability analysis was conducted to evaluate the environmental and social impact of Eleiko's current operations, and identify opportunities for improvement in their transition towards a circular business model.

Research

Research was conducted to understand customer needs, behaviors, and challenges related to purchasing training equipment. The findings provided valuable insights that helped shape service concepts capable of supporting both customer needs and Eleiko's transition towards a circular business model.

Interviews

An interview guide was created to make sure relevant and conceived data, successfully would be collected. The goal was to explore the purchase process and its pain points to understand what needed to be developed, and what may make the customers consider to not get a gym. Several interviews with relevant customers was conducted.

Compile interview data

The interview data was transcribed, ensuring conceived data was captured. Then the data was coded, which made it possible to identify patterns in customers behavior, expectations, understand needs and identify preferences. Then the data was visualized with themes and categories to make the data easier to understand.

Stakeholder map

The relevant stakeholders was identified by a stakeholder map. The purpose was to undertand which actors Eleiko currently collaborated with, as well as other actors that could be important for Eleiko’s future network in a circular business model.

Current state & Concepts

This phase focused on defining and developing service concepts based on the research findings. By mapping current and future customer journeys and creating service blueprints, opportunities for improvement were identified and translated into feasible service solutions.

Current customer journey map

Based on the research, a customer journey map was developed to visualize the customer’s experience with Eleiko's current purchasing process. The map includes a scenario, the customers experience, emotions, pain points and opportunities.

Concept definition methods

To generate concept ideas, we conducted a brainstorming method called brainwriting 6-3-5. Through this, ideas for potential services came out. After the brainstorming session, an idea evaluation was conducted to sort out some of the big amount of ideas. Then, context scenarios were developed for the final ideas to make them more clear and turned into a concept of an service.

Future customer journey map

Once two services have been created, future customers journey maps was created. The purpose of this was to be able to visually communicate how the developed services positively would transform the purchasing process for Eleiko’s customers.

Service blueprint

Once the concepts was finalized, a service blueprint was developed for each service. The purpose was to visualize the logistics and to outline the mandatory steps that both the customers and Eleiko would need to follow for these services to work in practice.

Result & Design Solutions

Strength as a Service

Value for Eleiko

The concept Strength as a Service attracts new customer groups who are not willing to make large investments in a gym, in the long term it can offer various equipment packages and ensure a steady inflow of products.

Strength as a Service is a rental service designed to support Eleiko's transition to a circular business model. By renting out equipment and ensuring it can be returned after use, Eleiko can keep products in circulation for longer. The service also enables the offering of new, refurbished, and used equipment, creating more affordable options for customers while reducing waste.

EleikoCollect

Value for Eleiko

The concept EleikoCollect enables Eleiko to collect equipment from customers who have purchased it. This allows Eleiko to potentially offer rental, leasing, or sales of used equipment in the future. Through this, it promotes a circular business model.

EleikoCollect is a concept where companies can return Eleiko gym equipment in exchange for compensation. This supports Eleiko’s circular business model by reclaiming and reusing equipment.

Customers with a business account can have their equipment valued via Eleiko’s website. After valuation, they receive an estimated value and can have their gym dismantled and, if desired, replaced with new equipment.

Customers with unused or stored equipment can use EleikoCollect without purchasing new items. The equipment is collected for free, and the customer receives a voucher.

Corporate Account

Value for Eleiko

Through corporate accounts, companies can establish closer contact with Eleiko, collect contracts, previous orders and other information in one place. Companies will also have the ability to report damages to products and gaining a better oversight of their payments.

A corporate account feature can be implemented on Eleiko's website to enable companies to gather information in one place. Through this, Eleiko can offer specific features and tools that are only available to corporate accounts. For example, customers could view completed orders, current service agreements, receive better support and report product damages to receive service for the product.

Potential effects

By enabling equipment rental and product recirculation, the concept could reduce waste, extend product lifespans, and create new revenue opportunities for Eleiko. It also has the potential to lower barriers to entry for customers who may find purchasing new equipment too costly.
